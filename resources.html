Resources

If you feel you need urgent support, please reach out to any of the services listed below

Immediate Local Crisis Support (Lancashire & Rossendale)

  • NHS Mental Health Crisis Support: Call 111 and select Option 2 (available 24/7 for urgent mental health support across Lancashire and Rossendale).
  • Lancashire Mental Health Crisis Line: Call 0800 953 0110 (24/7 dedicated line for urgent assessment, advice, and support).
  • Lancashire Wellbeing Helpline: Call 0800 915 4640 or text HELLO to 07860 022846 (Mon–Fri 7pm–11pm, Sat–Sun 12pm–Midnight for emotional support).

24/7 National Support & Text Lines

  • Samaritans: Call 116 123 (free, confidential, open 24/7, 365 days a year).
  • Shout Crisis Text Line: Text SHOUT to 85258 (free, confidential, 24/7 text support).
  • Childline (Under 19s): Call 0800 1111 (free, confidential, 24/7 support for children and young people).
  • YoungMinds Parents Helpline: Call 0808 802 5544 (Mon–Fri 9:30am–4pm for parents/carers worried about a young person).
  • CALM (Campaign Against Living Miserably): Call 0800 58 58 58 (5pm–midnight daily).

In a Life-Threatening Emergency

If you or someone else is in immediate physical danger, please call 999 or go to your nearest Hospital Emergency Department (A&E).
