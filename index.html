Welcome to Maple Counselling Services

A safe, confidential space across Rossendale & surrounding areas to pause, reflect, and move forward. Finding the right therapist can feel overwhelming when you are already dealing with a lot. You don't have to navigate it alone.

Therapy isn't about being told what to do; it’s a compassionate, collaborative space to get to know yourself better. Together, we can explore where you feel stuck, make sense of difficult patterns, and help you find your balance again. Whether you are coping with anxiety, depression, past trauma, relationship struggles, new diagnosis, or navigating neurodiversity, I am here to support you at your own pace.

This is a space where you can truly be yourself, without judgment, filter, or pressure. Whether that means unmasking, swearing, or managing tic conditions like Tourette's, you are welcome exactly as you are.

About Me

Hi, I’m Lindsey

Over the years, I’ve witnessed firsthand the challenges and barriers people encounter when searching for mental health support. Knowing how heavy and stressful that process can be, I am passionate about easing that strain by offering a compassionate, nurturing environment.

Sometimes, speaking to family or friends can be more complex. Having a dedicated, qualified counsellor allows you to express yourself freely and openly in a safe, completely neutral space, without any worry of personal ties or judgment.
