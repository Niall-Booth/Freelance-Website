Sessions

Here at Maple Counselling Services, your therapy journey is tailored entirely to you. I offer personalised support with flexible scheduling and no commitment to a fixed number or frequency of sessions, putting you fully in control of your care.


Sessions usually run for a minimum of 50 minutes; anything over this is free of charge, allowing time to comfortably integrate grounding techniques when needed. As an independent practice, I offer significantly shorter waiting times than larger providers like the NHS, helping you access support when you need it most. To suit your lifestyle and personal preferences, sessions are available face-to-face at local venues near you, in your own home, online via WhatsApp or Zoom, or by telephone.

What do I offer?

Come as you are

My sessions are relaxed and non-judgmental. Whether that means letting out a string of swear words, letting your tics happen naturally without masking, or bringing your pet along for extra comfort and grounding, you are free to be wholly yourself.

Integrative Approaches

I draw on integrative approaches to suit your personal goals. To help you get the most out of your therapy, I provide complimentary access to professional resources—including audio grounding tools, reflection guides, and progress trackers- that give you practical support both inside and outside our sessions.
